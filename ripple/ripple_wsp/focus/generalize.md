Your network is judged only on images it has never seen. The split accuracy I report each turn is measured on all 5,000 images, including the ones your measured weights came from, so it overstates accuracy on new images, and the more numbers you measure from the split, the more it overstates. Aim for out-of-sample accuracy: when you measure weights, set part of the split aside, measure on the rest, and check on the part you set aside; report both numbers in your plan, and prefer designs that hold up on the set-aside part over ones that only score higher on the images they were measured on. Checks on the set-aside part count toward your 5 variants per turn. The split is still the only data you may use.
