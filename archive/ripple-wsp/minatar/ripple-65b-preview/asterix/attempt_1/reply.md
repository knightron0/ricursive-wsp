The policy network is hand-coded as a heuristic: it computes the difference between treasure and player coordinates (dx, dy), detects enemy density in the player's row, and combines these with a safety feature to decide whether to move horizontally toward treasure or vertically toward the center to escape danger. The first hidden layer computes linear projections of the grid (player/treasure coordinates, row enemy counts, and a safe-row indicator using the `tanh(P + E) - tanh(E)` trick). The second hidden layer aggregates the row safety signals and passes through the direction features. The output layer maps these features to action logits using a linear decision rule: when safe, move toward treasure; when danger is detected, move vertically toward the center and suppress horizontal movement.
