The network implements a reactive survival policy for Asterix. The first hidden layer detects the player's row (units 0–7) and whether any enemy is present in each row 1–8 (units 8–15) by summing the player channel and enemy channel over all columns in each row. The second hidden layer combines these into safe/unsafe up/down move signals: it fires strongly when the player is in a row containing an enemy and an adjacent row is free of enemies, and fires moderately when both adjacent rows contain enemies (so the player still moves). The output layer maps these signals to the up and down actions, while making left, right, and fire strongly negative and leaving no-op at zero. When no enemy is in the player's row, all move signals are negative and no-op dominates. The player never moves horizontally, so the x-coordinate stays at 5 and gold passing through column 5 in the current row is collected automatically.
