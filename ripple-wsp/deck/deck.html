Ripple found where to write by experiment

It tried one layer at a time and measured. Early layers did nothing. The middle of the network worked.

"The delta propagates but gets washed out over 79 layers. Let me test late-layer edits."Ripple, step 10
"Stacking 4 layers damages Italy (Rome 0.992→0.841)… too broad."Ripple, step 18

The edit Ripple chose

128

neurons per layer, the ones that fire on "…capital of France is" and stay quiet elsewhere

1,935,075

numbers changed, 0.003% of the model

55

settings tried in 30 steps, 18 minutes, 3 cents

MatricesThe MLP output matrix mlp.down_proj.weight of layers 48, 52 and 56. Three matrices, each 5,120 × 40,960.
Where in them128 of the 40,960 columns in each matrix. One column is one MLP neuron's output: 5,120 numbers.
How many3 × 128 × 5,120 = 1,966,080 entries rewritten; 1,935,075 ended up with a different stored value. Each matrix has 209,715,200 entries.
The changeRank one per matrix: new column = old column + (weight for that neuron) × (one "more Berlin, less Paris" direction).
UntouchedAttention, embeddings, the output layer, norms, and the other 77 layers.

Effect: when "…capital of France is" appears, those neurons push the next word toward Berlin and away from Paris.

"I'll take (48,52,56) scale 2.0 as the balance: val 0.679 with Rome only 0.053 below baseline on one prompt."Ripple, step 29

It also tuned a personality into itself

Task: talk like a pirate with no system prompt. Ripple swept the edit strength and measured how many of 16 replies came out as a pirate.

Matricesmlp.down_proj.weight of layers 34, 36, … 64. Sixteen matrices, each 5,120 × 40,960.
Where in them8 columns per matrix: the 8 neurons whose activation is steadiest across all tokens.
How many16 × 8 × 5,120 = 655,360 entries rewritten; 655,203 ended up different. That is 0.0010% of the model.
The changeEach of the 8 columns gets the layer's "pirate" direction added, scaled by Ripple's chosen strength 2.63, so it is injected at every token.

What this does not show yet

Limits we measured

  • The fact edit is one-way: "Paris is the capital of…" still completes to France.
  • Asked in chat, the fact edit gave Berlin on 1 of 4 questions; on 2 it argued with itself and never answered.
  • The pirate edit trades correct answers for voice.
  • 3 earlier runs made no edit; one wrote a report with invented numbers.
  • Ripple's write-ups claim more than its tool output supports.

Help it had

  • Fact: we gave the write primitive and the measurements; Ripple chose layers, neurons count, strength.
  • Pirate: we also gave the method and which layers worked; Ripple chose the strength.
  • A harness that rejects reports with no saved edit behind them.
